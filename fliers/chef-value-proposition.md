# eazyfoods for Chefs – Value Proposition

**eazyfoods** — Authentic African groceries & meals, delivered

---

## Join as a Chef – Your Kitchen, Your Brand, More Orders

**eazyfoods** connects talented chefs with customers who want authentic African and international meals. List your cuisines, set your schedule, and get paid—all from one simple chef portal.

---

## Why chefs choose eazyfoods

- **Reach real customers** – Shoppers on eazyfoods are already looking for authentic meals and chef-made dishes.
- **One place for everything** – Profile, menu (cuisines), orders, promotions, and payouts in a single dashboard.
- **You're in control** – Set your service area, minimum order, prep time, and turn availability on or off anytime.
- **Get paid** – Track earnings and payouts, and choose how you accept payment (online and/or cash on delivery).
- **Stand out** – Run promotions and ads so more customers discover you on Top Chef Deals and across the site.
- **Build your reputation** – Customer reviews and ratings help new customers trust and choose you.

---

## What you get in the chef portal

| Feature | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Profile & brand** | Chef name, bio, photo, banner, cuisines, and service details. Show your story and what you cook. |
| **Cuisines (your menu)** | Add and edit your dishes with names, descriptions, prices, and images. Keep your menu up to date. |
| **Orders** | See new and past orders, update status, and manage prep and delivery from one place. |
| **Promotions** | Create deals and discounts (e.g. % off or fixed amount) to attract more orders. |
| **Ads & visibility** | Promote your brand on high-traffic pages (e.g. Top Chef Deals) so more customers find you. |
| **Reviews & ratings** | Read customer reviews and respond to build trust and improve your offering. |
| **Gallery** | Showcase your best dishes and your kitchen so customers know what to expect. |
| **Analytics & payouts** | View performance and earnings, and manage payout settings so you get paid on time. |
| **Chat & support** | Communicate with customers and get help from the eazyfoods team when you need it. |
| **Settings** | Service radius, minimum order, service fee, estimated prep time, and payment options—all configurable. |
