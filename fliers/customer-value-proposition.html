Authentic African Groceries & Meals, Delivered

eazyfoods is your one-stop place for authentic African groceries, ingredients, and chef-made meals. Order from local stores and talented chefs—delivered to your door or ready for pickup.

Why shop eazyfoods

  • Real African flavours – Groceries, spices, and ingredients you know and love, plus chef-made dishes from cooks who specialize in African and international cuisines.
  • Stores and chefs in one place – Browse groceries from verified vendors and custom meals from verified chefs. One cart, one checkout.
  • Deals that save – Top Market Deals and Top Chef Deals so you can discover new products and meals at great prices.
  • Delivery or pickup – Choose delivery to your address or pickup when it suits you. Quick, reliable service.
  • Trust and quality – Verified vendors and chefs, clear product details, and customer reviews so you know what you're getting.
  • Support when you need it – Questions about an order or a product? Our team is here to help.

What you can get

Groceries
Authentic African ingredients, spices, grains, sauces, and pantry staples from local stores.
Chef meals
Custom-prepared dishes from verified chefs—order by dish, choose servings, and get it delivered or pick it up.
Deals
Weekly deals on groceries and chef cuisines so you can try something new or stock up for less.
Meal plans
Curated meal options and plans from chefs and vendors (where available).
