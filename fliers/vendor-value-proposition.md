# eazyfoods for Vendors – Value Proposition

**eazyfoods** — Authentic African groceries & meals, delivered

---

## Sell on eazyfoods – Your Store, Your Products, More Customers

**eazyfoods** connects grocery and food vendors with customers who want authentic African ingredients and products. List your store, add your products, and manage orders—all from one simple vendor portal.

---

## Why vendors choose eazyfoods

- **Reach real customers** – Shoppers on eazyfoods are already looking for authentic African groceries and ingredients.
- **One place for everything** – Store profile, products, orders, inventory, and payouts in a single dashboard.
- **You're in control** – Set your delivery radius, product catalog, pricing, and stock. Update anytime.
- **Get paid** – Track earnings and payouts. See available balance and payout history in the portal.
- **Stand out** – Run promotions and appear on Top Market Deals so more customers discover you.
- **Build your reputation** – Customer reviews and ratings help new shoppers trust and choose your store.

---

## What you get in the vendor portal

| Feature | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Store & profile** | Business name, description, address, and store details. Show customers who you are. |
| **Products** | Add and edit products with names, descriptions, prices, images, and stock. Manage your catalog. |
| **Orders** | See new and past orders, update status (accept, picking, ready, delivered), and track payment. |
| **Inventory** | Low-stock alerts, stock tracking, and adjustment history so you never run out unexpectedly. |
| **Payouts** | View available balance, payout history, and commission breakdown. Get paid on time. |
| **Dashboard** | Today's orders, revenue, week/month stats, and quick actions at a glance. |
| **Promotions** | Create deals and discounts to attract more orders (where available). |
| **Reviews** | Read customer reviews and respond to build trust. |
| **Settings** | Delivery radius, business details, and account settings—all configurable. |
