# eazyfoods for Drivers – Value Proposition

**eazyfoods** — Authentic African groceries & meals, delivered

---

## Become a Delivery Driver – Flexible Hours, Competitive Pay

Deliver authentic African groceries and chef-made meals to customers in your area. We handle the orders and the app—you bring the wheels and the hustle.

---

## Why drive with eazyfoods

- **Flexible earnings** – Earn on every delivery. Get paid for the work you do, when you do it.
- **Work your hours** – Choose your own schedule. Drive mornings, evenings, or weekends—it's up to you.
- **Quick signup** – Simple application and fast approval. Get started and accept orders in no time.
- **Clear process** – See where to go, what to pick up, and where to deliver—all in the driver app.
- **Support when you need it** – Get help from the eazyfoods team if you have questions or run into issues.

---

## How it works

| Step | What you do |
|------|----------------|
| **1. Apply online** | Fill out a short application with your details and vehicle information. |
| **2. Get approved** | We review your application and get in touch within a few business days. |
| **3. Start delivering** | Complete a quick onboarding, then start accepting delivery requests. |

---

## What you need

- Valid driver's licence
- Vehicle (car, bike, or scooter depending on your area)
- Smartphone with our driver app
- Ability to lift and carry grocery bags
